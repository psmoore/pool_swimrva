Competition Pool

SwimRVA is proud to offer a beautiful, indoor 50 meter competition pool—the Collegiate School Aquatics Center. This pool was used in the 2008 Olympic trials where 9 World records and 21 American records were broken. This state of the art pool has two movable bulk heads and can be set up either 25 yards (SCY) or 50 meters (LCM) in length. The breathtaking views from our mezzanine are a wonderful sight to see.

Switch pools with the Pools filter, and move between weeks with the arrows. On a phone the same calendar opens as a list.

What is different from the live page

  • The four week-by-week tables are replaced by one calendar that always shows the current week. There is no “filter by week” dropdown to maintain and no stale week left behind.
  • It says which lanes, not just how many. The live page says “6 lanes”; the calendar can show lanes 1–6 by name in the Day view.
  • All three pools are in the same place. Competition, Instructional and Therapy are a dropdown rather than three separate pages.
  • The September closures list above is redundant: closures are events, so they appear in the schedule itself.
  • Navigation links point at the live SwimRVA site. Everything else here is unofficial.
